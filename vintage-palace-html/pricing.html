House rules

The fine print, set in large type

Booking & advance

₹75,000 at booking; the remainder 3 days before the event date. Amounts are non-refundable but adjustable within 3 months for any occasion at the venue.

Décor policy

Décor is executed by the venue's panel decorators (from ₹30,000). Outside decorator panels are not permitted, which keeps quality and teardown discipline consistent.

Catering

The house kitchen and caterers serve veg and non-veg menus; outside food is generally not brought in. Final menu and per-plate pricing are fixed after tasting.

DJ & music

The venue doesn't run a house DJ — bring your own with the required licence. Amplified sound respects neighbourhood late-night norms.

Alcohol

Serving alcohol requires a valid liquor licence arranged by the host, with standard corkage norms thereafter.

What's not allowed

Firecrackers are not permitted. Hawan is warmly permitted; baarat and overnight weddings are welcome.

Power & electricity

Hall electrical usage and generator charges are billed at actuals (diesel beyond the base figure). Power backup is on-site throughout your slot.

Rooms

Two complimentary fully-AC changing/bridal rooms with attached bathrooms; additional guest rooms on campus (standard doubles from about ₹1,000/night).

Taxes & payment

18% GST applies on F&B; venue rental terms as quoted. Pay by cash, bank transfer, cheque, or credit/debit card.

FAQ

The ten questions everyone asks

How many guests fit comfortably?

600–3,000: the Palace Hall seats 400 (700 floating); the Royal Lawn seats 950 (1,500 floating). Combined evening events have run near 3,000.

What's the total budget shape?

Rental from ₹2.7L (hall) or ₹3.5L full day (lawn) + décor from ₹30K + food per plate + 18% GST on F&B + generator actuals. The events desk gives a single payable sheet before you commit.

Can we visit before booking?

Always. Site visits run daily 11 AM–10 PM. Call +91 91603 74743 or use the contact form.

Is the date adjustable if plans change?

Yes — payments are non-refundable but adjustable within 3 months for any occasion at the venue, subject to availability.

Do you allow outside caterers?

The venue's in-house kitchen handles catering to hold quality and hygiene steady. Special-diet and multi-cuisine requests are welcomed at the briefing.

Which documents do we bring for booking?

A basic ID and the advance amount. For liquor service, the host arranges a valid liquor licence. For your own DJ, a music licence.

Is parking really enough for 1,000+ guests?

On-premises parking holds 100–110 cars, and the main-road location keeps drop-and-park queues moving. For very large evenings, arrive early — as with most venues in the old city.

How early should we book for muhurat dates?

Muhurat-season Saturdays pull date requests months ahead. Enquire at least 3–4 months out for peak dates; off-season flexibility extends to the 3-month adjustability window.
