The Palace Hall

The Royal Lawn

A wide green canvas for mandaps, jaan entrances, live baraat with the dhol, and receptions that breathe under the open sky.

The lawn is where the palace shows its range — daytime haldi, golden-hour nikaah photographs, night-time sangeet drones. The in-house decorating panel turns it around for any theme inside hours.

  • Mandap & stage setups included in packages
  • Hawan and baarat both welcomed
  • Overnight weddings permitted (event till ~6 AM)
  • Marigold, floral, and royal theme panels
  • Separate dining annex keeps the floor clear
  • Easy guest flow from ribbon-gate to stage

Behind the scenes

Thoughtful spaces for the people hosting

Because a smooth celebration is won long before the first guest arrives.

✦

Bridal & Changing Suites

2 complimentary, fully air-conditioned rooms with attached bathrooms for the couple and close family — ready from morning prep through the last outfit change. Additional guest rooms of 3 available on the premises.

✦

Separate Dining Annex

An independent dining wing — repeatedly named in reviews — so dinner queues never cross the greeting line, and the hall stays dressed and camera-perfect.

✦

Parking & Arrival

On-premises parking for 100–110 cars on the main Golconda Road corridor, with transport access guests call “easy.” Additional off-peak on-street support for peak-season overflow.
