A Golconda Road Landmark

Traditional hospitality, dressed for today

WedMeGood calls The Vintage Palace “a fine relic of traditional Indian hospitality, blended with the technologically advanced facilities of the modern hospitality industry.” Nothing sums up the house style better.

Set beside Mahaboob Pride Function Hall on Karwan’s main road, the palace sits in the old city’s beating heart — the Fort on the horizon, temples and masjids around the corner, and easy transport on every side. Guests describe evenings under its chandeliers as “amazing… everyone loved it.”
