Also honest

Two things worth knowing

One

Parking at peak evenings

The on-site lot holds 110 vehicles — generous by old-city standards, but on the busiest muhurat nights the front rows fill early. Arrive a touch early, or let the team stage the gate for drop-and-park flow. This is simply what hosts should expect at peak times.

Two

These halls are not air-conditioned

The halls are high, arched and well-ventilated, with coolers on the floor and additional arrangements possible. If you expect a sealed, chilled ballroom, call ahead and discuss cooling for your date — the team will show you what the halls feel like on a similar evening.
