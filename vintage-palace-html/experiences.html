One Campus, Every Celebration

Eighteen event types seen on one campus — moods shifting under the same chandeliers, season after season.

Occasions

Choose the mood, we bring the grace

i

The Wedding

Multi-day ceremonies with halls, lawn, and a décor panel that reads a mood board like a manuscript. Muhurat dates get priority holds.

ii

The Reception

Stage greeting lines, a lit-up arch entrance, and a dining annex so dinner flows while hosts photograph and toast.

iii

Sangeet & DJ Night

Bring your own DJ (with licence) — reviews say the hall "was built to dance in." Amplified sound, light rigs, late finish.

iv

Haldi & Mehendi

Tent courtyards on the lawn, dupatta lines, boardwalk seating for the aunties, and a wash-ready annex right behind.

v

Engagement & Ring Ceremony

Intimate seating around a dressed stage for 200–400; elders' blessing seats and photo-friendly lighting by default.

vi

Nikaah Programme

Reverently arranged mehfil seating, coordinated halal catering, and quiet family suites for the elders' sessions.

vii

Birthday, Naming & Baby Showers

Cake tables under the arch, soft balloon artistry on the panel's résumé, and rooms for the kids to nap.

viii

Corporate & Seminars

Projector and TV screens, stage with mic, boardroom-quiet AC hall, and tea-time in the annex. Conferences to prom fares.

ix

Anniversaries & Reunions

Milestone reels on the TVs, memory lanes of photos, and a turnout plan for three generations to sit well.

Your day, our way

A rhythm that keeps the day kind

Morning · from 10:00 AM

Ready & dressed

Bridal suite opens with the AC on. Décor team finishes the mandap while you sip your chai. Haldi and mehendi unfold on the lawn.

Afternoon

Ceremonies & lunch

Rituals at the stage. Guests move to the dining annex in waves — no queue crossings, air conditioning wherever it counts.

Evening · till late

Lights, stage, farewell

Chandeliers up, DJ on, chandelier-glow photographs — then a comfortable overnight wind-down with family rooms.
