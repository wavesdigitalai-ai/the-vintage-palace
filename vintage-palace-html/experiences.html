Your day

A rhythm that keeps the day kind

Morning

10:00 am · doors open, decor done

Booking slots open at 10 am. The decorator finishes the mandap while the family drinks chai in the changing rooms. Haldi unfolds first on the day sheet.

Site visits run 11 am – 10 pm daily, so daytime walk-throughs are easy to arrange around work hours.

Afternoon

Rituals, then the dining hall

Wedding rites conclude at the stage. Guests move to dinner in waves; the greeting line stays clear for portraits and têtes-à-têtes.

Well-ventilated halls plus coolers keep the afternoon humane — ask the team about threshold cooling on your date.

Evening

Chandeliers, spotlight, farewell

Overnight weddings are permitted, and the garden knows how to glow — but the house respects its neighbours: no late-night music, so plan the last dance before the night hush.

Dessert at the farewell, rest for the elders in the rooms, then families turn in on campus.
